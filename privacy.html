Kitsune Budget – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Kitsune Budget (“the app”) is a personal budget and asset tracker made and run by an individual developer (“KitsuneChess”, “the developer”). This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

1. The short version

  • The app does not connect to your bank accounts or credit cards.
  • You do not create an account or sign in.
  • The budget data you enter (transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and so on) is stored only on your device. The developer does not receive it and cannot see it.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information, described below.

2. Data you enter

Everything you type into the app, such as income, expenses, account balances, notes, budgets and saving goals, is saved in your device’s local storage. It is not sent to any server of the developer, because the app has no server for your data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted from your device. You can also delete it at any time with “Reset data” in Settings.

If you use “Save backup” or “Export to spreadsheet (CSV)”, the app creates a file on your device and lets you choose where to save or share it. That file is under your control. Please keep it somewhere safe, because it contains your budget data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app displays banner ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. The developer does not receive your personal information from this process.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app asks for your consent using Google’s consent tool before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in Settings (“Ad privacy choices”), where this is available.
  • You can reset or turn off personalized ads in your device’s Google settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Ads are not shown in Kids mode.

4. Information not collected

The developer does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or financial account information. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

5. Children

The app is intended for adults. It includes a “Kids mode”, a simple allowance book that a parent or guardian can set up for a child on the same device. A passcode set by the adult keeps a child from switching to the adult screens. Kids mode stores its data only on the device and does not show ads. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Disclaimer

Kitsune Budget is a personal record-keeping tool. It is not a tax-filing tool and does not provide tax, legal or financial advice. The calculations and messages in the app are only a guide.

The app is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. Because your data is stored only on your device, it can be lost, for example if the device is lost, reset or damaged, or if the app is uninstalled. Please save backups regularly. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the developer is not responsible for any loss or damage, including financial loss or loss of data, that results from using the app or from being unable to use it. You are responsible for your own financial decisions.

7. Data security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on your device. It is recommended to use a screen lock and keeping your backup files in a safe place.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
